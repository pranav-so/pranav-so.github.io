FONT SIZES ON THE SITE
======================

All sizes are set in style.css. Search for the name in brackets to find
the line to change.

Font everywhere: Times New Roman (serif)


What                                   Size    Bold?   Where in style.css
-------------------------------------  ------  ------  ------------------
Your name under the photo              30px    yes     h1
Section headings ("Writing", "Now")    22px    yes     h2
Everything else: paragraphs, links,    16px    no      body
  the footer line at the bottom, the
  bulleted writing list, dates
Small grey ALL-CAPS label              11px    yes     .label
  ("WORKING ON")


Widths (these set how wide the text runs)
-----------------------------------------
Photo                                  200px wide, about 229px tall
Left column (photo, name)              200px wide
Gap between the two columns            5.6rem (= about 90px)
Right column (main text)               36rem (= 576px) at most

The white space left over on a wide screen becomes the margins on either
side. To make the text narrower (bigger margins), lower 36rem in the
grid-template-columns line near the bottom of style.css. To make it wider,
raise it.

On phones the two columns stack into one, at most 38rem (= 608px) wide.
